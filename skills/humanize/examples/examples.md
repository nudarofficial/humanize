## Rewrite: formal paragraph (English)

Input:

> The Burj Khalifa, standing at 828 metres, serves as a testament to Dubai's ambitious vision, symbolizing its rapid transformation. Completed in 2010, it plays a pivotal role in the city's tourism landscape, attracting millions of visitors annually.

Output:

> The Burj Khalifa is 828 metres tall and was completed in 2010. It is a major part of Dubai's tourism and draws millions of visitors each year.

## Rewrite: social post (English)

Input:

> I’m thrilled to share that I’ve completed the Adobe Digital Marketing certificate — a pivotal milestone in my journey. 🚀
>
> This program wasn't just about tools; it was about mindset. It underscored the importance of data-driven storytelling, highlighting how brands can foster meaningful connections. Additionally, I gained valuable insights into SEO, social media, and analytics.
>
> - **Strategy:** Learning to align content with audience needs.
> - **Analytics:** Turning data into decisions.
>
> Despite its challenges, this journey has been transformative. Here's the thing: marketing is no longer about reach. It's about resonance, rather than raw numbers.
>
> In conclusion, I hope this helps anyone considering the course! Let me know if you'd like a more detailed breakdown.

Output:

> I finished the Adobe Digital Marketing certificate.
>
> It covered SEO, social media and analytics. The part that stuck with me is using data to decide which story to tell, and how brands build real connections with people. It wasn't easy in places.
>
> The biggest shift in how I think is about reach. It's the first number everyone checks, and on its own it says very little about whether anyone cared. Resonance is what I'm trying to measure now.
>
> If you're thinking about taking it, message me and I'll tell you more.

## Review: social post (English)

Input: the social post from the second example above.

Output:

- "a pivotal milestone in my journey": inflated importance and AI vocabulary. Strong.
- "wasn't just about tools; it was about mindset": negative parallelism. Strong.
- "highlighting how brands can foster meaningful connections": shallow "-ing" tail with stock vocabulary. Strong.
- "Here's the thing:": stock social-media hook. Medium.
- "rather than raw numbers": the reversed "Y rather than X" frame. Medium.
- "**Strategy:**" and "**Analytics:**": bullets that open with a bold label. Medium.
- "In conclusion, I hope this helps": wrap-up ending plus a chatbot sign-off. Strong.
- Em dash, curly apostrophes and a rocket emoji: formatting habits. Weak on their own.

Several strong signs together; this reads as AI-written.
