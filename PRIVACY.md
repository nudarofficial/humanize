# Privacy

Humanize collects no data. It's a set of instructions and reference files that your AI agent reads on your own device or account. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no network calls, and the optional checker script runs locally without network access.

Whatever you paste for rewriting is handled by the AI product you use it with (for example Claude), under that product's privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/nudarofficial/humanize/issues
